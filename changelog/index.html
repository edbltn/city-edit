2026-07-23

Differential block heat — net-against blocks go cold

Block heat was total deduped activity (up + down), so a net-against block glowed as hot as a net-for one — opposition read as popularity. Heat is now the signed differential (up − down) of each block's top-ranked proposal, ranked by differential (topProposalDiffs = max across the block's vote types): positive rides the warm ramp, zero (cancelled signal) is invisible, negative descends a new per-theme cold arm ending almost blue (bikes: steel → vivid blue; transit/waterfront swing violet; light themes tint slate). Feature-state heat is ∈ [−1, 1] with a log denominator per arm — negatives normalize against their own tight floor (NEG_HEAT_FULL_SCALE 10) so organic opposition reaches deep cold beside bulk-import positives — and a −0.001 stop pins mild negatives to cold so they can never interpolate toward warm. Legend gradient spans the full spectrum, zero at 25%. Client-only change (derived from breakdowns already in every payload). Verified live on nyc-bikes: 24,363 negative / 17,881 positive blocks, countered avenues blue against warm greenways; 258 tests pass.

Layer-anchor failsafes — the map can no longer stay misaligned

Screenshots caught the white route layer offset from the block heat — differently at each zoom — then healing on its own. The projection math was fine; both zoom-animation "rides" (the GL container transform and the heat/highlight canvas transform) re-anchor through a fragile chain — MapLibre's camera resynced only by Leaflet's final move event, the canvas repaint on a bare rAF (which freezes entirely in hidden/occluded windows), and mid-animation paints from WS deltas baking inconsistent transforms into the bitmap. On block maps the bitmap's painted-camera record (drawStateRef) was never even written. Five failsafes: paints are deferred while a zoom is in flight, the painted camera is stamped on every paint, the rAF repaint gets a 200 ms timer backstop, an ensureAnchored() drift check on every move frame + visibility restore repaints synchronously when bitmap zoom ≠ map zoom, and a MapLibre reconcile() on zoomend/moveend/visibility clears leftover ride transforms, re-jumps the camera unconditionally, and forces one GL frame. Each is a cheap no-op when nothing drifted.

Citibike import correction — a bike-legality graph votes back

Imported Citibike rides are pedestrianized on purpose — the gap between how a pedestrian moves and where a bike may go IS the vote — but the ingest upvoted the whole foot path, over-crediting corridors that already carry bikes (worst where lanes already exist, near stations). Graph-first blocks — membership decides the polygons, and no point on the map is a dead zone

The central-park test exposed two structural defects in Layer-2 blocks: heatmap gaps (voted edges whose polygon never existed — 2,450 unmapped on test-cp) and edges mapped to polygons they don't even touch (10,336 nearest-snapped). Both came from generating polygons first and mapping edges into them geometrically. A new one-pass builder inverts the premise: edges are grouped topologically first (junction clusters + corridors severed at junctions, with the old merge pass's stub/fake-junction rules run as a pre-geometry fixpoint), and each block's polygon is generated from exactly its own member edges — 100% coverage and 100% edge∩polygon overlap by construction, audited at build time. Client-side, the shared hover/click/drag resolver lost its off-polygon 4px radius gate: every hover and click resolves to the closest node/edge and its encompassing block — verified by a 280-point live grid sweep (280/280 resolve, 0 block mismatches) and click tests in former dead zones. 2026-07-08

Node/edge parity — the ends of every segment belong to their nodes
