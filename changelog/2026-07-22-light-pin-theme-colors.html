Frontend

1 · Light-mode top-proposal pins read grey — and selected pins read blank

Symptom

On light map styles (terracotta / trees / plum) the top-proposal pin glow rings rendered as shades of grey, tan and near-black — never the map's identity color. A selected pin was worse: black double ring on a white fill, no visible theme at all.

Root cause

  • Pins sample their glow color from the map's heat ramp — correct on dark basemaps, whose additive (screen-blend) ramps are vivid against dark paper. But the light styles' ramps are engineered for multiply blending: they darken the basemap, so their stops run pale sand → muted brick → near-black plum/indigo. Painted flat on a thin pin ring (measured live: hottest pin rgb(92,32,82), mid ranks rgb(214,152,106)) they read as greys and tans — the terracotta accent #C0674A appeared nowhere.
  • Selected pins on light paper pair an ink-black double ring with the paper fill; the heat tint (26% of an already-pale ramp color) was imperceptible, and a zero-vote selection has no tint at all — hence “just white bg”.

What changed

  • Accent-anchored pin ramp on light basemaps. New buildPinRampStops(style): dark basemaps keep the heat ramp verbatim; light basemaps interpolate pale-accent-tint (lowest rank) → pure accent (hottest pin, at HEAT_PEAK_POS) → ink-deepened accent (unused tip). Rank still reads as color depth, and every pin wears the theme. Both pin sites (PBTP squares + RBTP diamonds) switched; the canvas heatmap keeps the true multiply ramp.
  • Guaranteed accent tint on selected fills (light only). .is-selected fills now mix a baseline 12% of the glow color — falling back to var(--accent) for zero-vote selections — plus the existing heat-scaled share, so a selected pin is never a bare white box under its ink ring.
