Getting it made

Seven steps, in order

Route Fixed cost Sales tax / VAT Verdict

  1. Build the files

    cd tools/merch && ./env/bin/python build_merch.py --previews --clean writes ten PNGs to out/. Deterministic — the same command always produces the same bytes.

  2. Open the shop and pick the blanks

    Create a free Fourthwall shop. Choose a black tee, a natural tee, and a white glossy 11oz mug. The mug art is a dark full-bleed wrap for a white blank — that is not the same thing as ordering a black mug.

  3. Upload one product per design

    Choose "fit to print area" for each. The canvases are already the right physical size, so nothing needs scaling. Leave the tee art transparent — flattening it onto a black rectangle prints a black box on a black shirt at roughly triple the ink cost.

  4. Order a sample of every design

    The step to not skip. Whether 30% grey survives DTG on black, whether the ramp's purple end reads as purple or as mud, and how the wrap meets the mug handle are all things a screen cannot tell you.

    Check on arrival

    Crossword cell rules unbroken · desire-path kites still cyan and red, not washed · heat ramp showing four distinct steps · mug seam with no white sliver at the handle.

  5. Price from the sample

    Tees around $32–36, mugs around $20 — roughly 2.5× base, and in line with other civic-project shops. Bundle the Crossword tee with the Clues mug at $46; they were designed as one object.

  6. Point shop.cityedit.org at the store

    Add a CNAME at the registrar to the host Fourthwall gives you, then connect the subdomain in its dashboard. One DNS record, zero deploys.

    Do not add it to terraform

    custom_domains in terraform/main.tf is for hostnames Cloud Run answers, and a Cloud Run domain mapping needs DNS pointed at Google — the opposite of what this hostname needs. This is why the shop is a CNAME and not an nginx 301 like donate.cityedit.org.

  7. Turn on the nav link

    Set SHOP_URL = "https://shop.cityedit.org" in NavRail.tsx and deploy the client. The tote glyph is hidden while that constant is empty, so the link cannot ship ahead of the store.

What the files already handle

So you don't rediscover it

Ink opacity is floored at 30%. The logo draws empty crossword cells at 15%; over a black garment, DTG lays that over a white underbase and it silts up into a muddy near-black. 30% is the faintest mark that survives fabric.

No stroke thinner than 6px at 300 DPI. DTG prints thin lines broken or not at all; 4px is the usual floor and the lightest rule in the set clears it.

Text is outlined at build time. No print shop ever needs Red Hat Mono installed, and no glyph can silently fall back to Courier on someone else's machine.

Physical size is stamped into each PNG. Uploaders read the pHYs chunk to sanity-check dimensions, and the renderer doesn't write one on its own.

Confirm the 2475 × 1155 mug template against Fourthwall's own download before the first order — it is the common 11oz spec but not a universal one. It lives in one place: MUG_W/MUG_H in designs.py.
